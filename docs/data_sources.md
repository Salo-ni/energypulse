# EnergyPulse Data Sources

## 1. Electricity and Energy

Potential sources:

- U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)
- International Energy Agency (IEA)
- Public electricity datasets

Potential data:

- Electricity generation
- Electricity demand
- Generation by energy source
- Renewable generation
- Electricity prices

## 2. Oil

Potential sources:

- EIA
- Public market datasets

Potential data:

- Brent crude
- WTI crude
- Historical oil prices

## 3. Natural Gas

Potential sources:

- EIA
- Public market datasets

Potential data:

- Natural gas prices
- Regional prices
- Historical price movements

## 4. Weather

Initial candidate:

- Open-Meteo

Potential data:

- Temperature
- Wind speed
- Precipitation
- Solar-related weather indicators

## Source Selection Criteria

Final data sources will be selected based on:

- API availability
- Data quality
- Licensing
- Historical coverage
- Update frequency
- Geographic coverage
- Reliability
- Ease of automated ingestion
